PART 3 · WHAT DOES IT MEAN?

A metaphor does NOT mean it is real.

A metaphor says one thing IS another thing to show a special quality.

We don't mean they are really the same.

THING A + IS + THING B → QUALITY
Ali is a lion.

Ali → lion → brave

He is very brave.
